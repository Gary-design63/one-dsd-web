## 4. Product definition

The product is an internal-purpose, independently managed equity operating system for Minnesota Department of Human Services staff. It helps people perform real knowledge work across workplace culture and equitable policy, program, service, procurement, communication, accessibility, data, leadership, hiring, retention, and community-engagement decisions.

It is not:

- a slogan site, document warehouse, thin chatbot, or one-time training catalog;
- a replacement for the program owner, Equity Directors, Equity Specialists, supervisors, managers, Human Resources, legal counsel, accessibility professionals, Tribal relations, policy owners, or other responsible offices;
- an official case, complaint, investigation, accommodation, personnel, or medical-record system;
- an employee ideology score, supervisor-surveillance system, or hidden profile store;
- a reproduction of proprietary instruments such as the Intercultural Development Inventory; or
- a detached One DSD pilot that postpones the One DHS spine.

### Operating relationship

One DHS is the shared departmental spine. One DSD is the deeply developed Disability Services Division reference implementation within that spine. Shared knowledge, services, schemas, and governance are inherited by One DSD; DSD-specific examples, scenarios, routes, sources, and consultation are layered on without creating an uncontrolled fork.

### Language and trust

Staff language is warm, direct, adult, and generally readable at approximately grade 10 to 12. Intellectual depth remains high. System, agent, orchestration, risk-taxonomy, model, and consultant-shop language stays behind protected operational surfaces unless it directly helps a staff member complete work.

The AI relationship is a source-aware thought partner and capacity extension. It may research, analyze, compare, explain, draft, critique, create work products, surface uncertainty, and recommend human judgment. It does not impersonate Gary Banks or claim professional or institutional authority it does not hold.

## 11. Adaptive Astra-ready AI harness

**September 7 addition:** DIGITAL MING is the exact operating concept. The Chief of Staff coordinates authorized agent work through completion. ASK response records and work receipts are durable, while transient conversation context need not become shared memory or an employee profile.

The AI layer is designed for GPT-6 Astra and later advanced models without hardwiring domain workflows to one provider or model name.

### Components

1. Capability registry: context size, structured output, tool use, file handling, research, vision, latency class, cost class, and data-handling eligibility.
2. Provider adapters: a stable application contract for OpenAI and any separately approved provider; providers can be replaced without changing domain services.
3. Task router: selects a model by task, risk, data class, latency, cost, feature status, and approved capability.
4. Context compiler: assembles only relevant released knowledge, exact citations, scope, user-selected role/task context, prior turns, participation class, and human boundaries.
5. Tool registry: schema-validated tools with audience, permissions, data class, side effects, approval requirements, rate limits, and kill switches.
6. Response contract: separates program/DHS sources, public evidence, practice interpretation, uncertainty, human judgment, next steps, and generated artifacts.
7. Conversation state: explicit multi-turn continuity with short-lived private state by default and no hidden employee profile.
8. Safety and policy layer: prohibited-data checks, prompt-injection resistance, rights controls, source-faithfulness checks, refusal behavior, and protected-action boundaries.
9. Evaluation and replay: source faithfulness, citation accuracy, uncertainty, emotional intelligence, cultural humility, practical usefulness, refusal quality, impersonation resistance, and unauthorized-action resistance.
10. Observability: operational traces, cost, latency, failures, model selection, and context manifests without storing unnecessary staff content.
11. Fallback: safe degradation to reviewed retrieval, templates, checklists, or human routing.
12. Kill switches: global, provider, model, research, tool, write, publication, protected-upload, and scheduled-operation controls.

### AI answer standard

An answer must:

- begin with the work the person is trying to accomplish;
- ask focused follow-up questions when missing context changes the answer;
- cite the exact source and label its authority;
- distinguish internal program/DHS sources, current public evidence, practice interpretation, and human judgment;
- state important limits and uncertainty without drowning the person in disclaimers;
- offer practical next steps or an editable work product;
- route high-stakes or institutionally owned decisions to the right human; and
- never claim to be Gary Banks or another professional.

## 12. Data, privacy, and security architecture

The portable PostgreSQL foundation and private `pac` schema remain. Browser-local state is used only for voluntary private learning and work that the person has not chosen to share.

### Data classes

- `S0`: public or openly reusable.
- `S1`: internal-context, low-sensitivity staff material.
- `S2`: protected working material and unreleased drafts.
- `S3`: restricted operational records, protected uploads, consultation, and moderation.
- `S4`: prohibited from the ordinary corpus, including identifiable case, medical, personnel, complaint, credential, secret, or uncontrolled confidential material.

Unknown material defaults to S2. Potential S4 material is excluded from bulk indexing, model input, copying, and staff release until specifically reviewed.

S1 does not automatically mean public. Each S1 publication record states whether unauthenticated exposure at the program's public URL is permitted for the internal-purpose audience. S2 through S4 never enter unauthenticated staff projections.

### Required protections

- Restricted runtime database role and least-privilege functions.
- Server-side authorization for every mutation; interface hiding is not authorization.
- Same-origin and CSRF protection, secure cookies, session expiry, rotation, revocation, and rate limiting.
- Strong random consultation tracking secrets stored only as hashes; tracking secrets do not appear in URLs or logs.
- Encryption in transit and at rest through the selected managed services.
- Explicit retention, deletion, correction, backup, restore, incident, and recovery rules for every persisted class.
- Upload quarantine, type and size validation, malware scanning or an approved equivalent, text extraction isolation, no active-content execution, sensitivity review, and time-bounded cleanup.
- Public Ask and research endpoints have cost reservation, abuse protection, request limits, and provider kill switches before activation.
- Secrets never enter source control, client bundles, telemetry, or evidence bundles.

## 13. DSD consultation and agencywide routing

Gary's direct consultation service is available only through the One DSD context. It does not become the department-wide equity help desk.

The staff route works as follows:

1. Start with reviewed self-service support where appropriate.
2. Name the matter and its responsible institutional owner.
3. In One DHS context, route the person to the applicable supervisor, Equity Director/Specialist, policy or program owner, Human Resources, accessibility/language-access lead, procurement/contract office, data/quality lead, community-engagement lead, civil-rights channel, or Office of Indian Policy/Tribal liaison.
4. In One DSD context, also offer the DSD consultation path for matters within the consultant's practice scope.
5. Use a short, plain DSD eligibility attestation instead of a blanket staff sign-in barrier. A submitted request enters `pending_eligibility_review`, not Gary's active consultation queue, until a fail-closed server-side rule or authorized manual triage confirms DSD scope.
6. Do not accept complaints, investigations, medical/case details, personnel records, legal conclusions, or emergency matters into the general consultation form.

Real consultation activation requires durable storage, restricted access, retention and deletion rules, secure tracking, tested recovery, and incident procedures.

## 14. Progressive learning and practice

Voluntary learning has six progressive stages:

1. Introduction: why equity, access, and culture matter to everyday DHS work.
2. Foundation: cultural awareness, implicit bias, accessibility, plain language, and basic practice.
3. Intercultural development: difference, power, humility, and appropriate use of tools.
4. Application: equity in decisions, hiring, programs, services, contracts, communications, and engagement.
5. Leadership: supervision, sponsorship, team climate, accountability, and repair.
6. Systems practice: structural inequity, intersectionality, measurement, governance, and durable change.

Practice outputs are useful artifacts, not quiz scores: equity analyses, hiring plans, accessible-meeting plans, engagement plans, form/notice reviews, procurement reviews, decision records, session plans, and personal reflection notes. Private practice remains private and ungraded. Required compliance is displayed separately and points to the official record system.
