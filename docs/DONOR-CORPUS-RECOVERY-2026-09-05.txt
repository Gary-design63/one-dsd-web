Candidate payload

Every candidate has two representations in one revision payload.

Release posture

The source item records the owner’s approval for ingestion. This is not publication approval.

Every source and candidate revision enters eight pending review queues: language alignment, factual currentness, accessibility, scope, placement, rights and consent, community representation, and legal-policy review.

All recovered content items are restricted and all recovered sources use consultant access. The import produces zero publication decisions. Promotion of collection membership still does not publish any item.
