## Source checks

Primary research, author-hosted manuscripts, journal records, and research abstracts were checked through web retrieval on the date above. Some publisher records expose only abstracts or previews; the course does not claim full-text review where unavailable. Summaries remain within what the accessible record supports. Teaching cases and practice designs are original fictional applications, not reported study results or official DHS policy. Sources are linked in every course pack.

## Authoring and privacy

- Four substantive lessons per course, each with three observable objectives, a distinct fictional case, three reasoned scenario choices with one recommended response, a knowledge check, private reflection, and applied practice.
- Each course ends with flashcards that rehearse its job-aid distinctions. Job aids provide topic-specific steps plus a private practice-note structure.
- Open access and voluntary participation; no completion gates, identity profiling, inferred IDI orientation, employee scores, or claims of required training credit.
- No new audio or video was fabricated. Existing photographic cover reused and visually inspected: five colleagues around a conference table. Alt text accurately describes the image rather than inferring identities.
- Governance has an empty reviewer list; no fabricated human review. The recorded review date is the authoring/source-check date. Future research or staff identification of unclear examples triggers review.
- Local authoring is complete; integration, build, browser verification, and any deployment are tracked by the coordinating task separately.
