## Questions with distinct provenance

- The preserved resource pn-equity-in-practice, “Equity in Practice: six perspectives and seven questions,” is in lib/content/corpus.ts. Its six perspectives and seven-question wording remain intact.
- The eight companion questions in lib/program/equity.ts were authored as the September 8, 2026 program synthesis of the published DHS policy, Minnesota Equity Analysis Tool, and GARE approach. docs/OPERATIONALIZING-EQUITY.md explains them as companion questions, not an official replacement sequence.
- A historical planning reference named KS-002, “Eight Equity Questions to Ask Before a Decision Is Final,” was identified by the source-recovery review. Its complete authored body has not been established in the reviewed material. The September 8 synthesis is not asserted to be that recovered source.

The shared themes are examining a decision, authority and affected people, evidence and assumptions, possible changes, responsibility, and learning from what happens. That conceptual relationship does not establish textual identity, authorship, or recovery of a missing source. Neither the seven-question resource nor the eight-question synthesis changes an existing DHS policy obligation.
