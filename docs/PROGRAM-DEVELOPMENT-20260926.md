# Program-wide development: implementation record

The owner approved the complete program-wide plan on September 26, 2026 and subsequently authorized deployment after opening the local pathway preview. The application and offline SharePoint counterpart share the same developmental model and per-resource mapping.

## Intended change

Guide staff from a meaningful question through recognizing differences, examining assumptions, practicing an alternative, applying it with appropriate support, and reflecting on what happened. The organizational starting point of widespread Minimization is the owner's design premise, not a new assessment result or an individual classification. Personal growth, relationships, cultural understanding, and changes to workplace practices all belong in the program.

## Implementation

- `lib/program/development.json` supplies six learning-and-action stages, five complete pathways, social-learning prompts, a logic model, and research rationale. These stages are not IDI assessment categories.
- `lib/program/development-membership.json` gives 325 distinct published items explicit developmental purposes. Most have one or two relevant pathways; the DSD Day 1 checklist connects to Start. Publication readers continue to determine whether an item is visible in a staff scope.
- Home, Start, About, and Learning link directly to the pathways. Course overviews, lessons, and resource pages provide content-specific guidance. Route-level guidance connects Areas, communities, leadership, support, practice, and other staff entry points.
- Pathways offer open access to all six stages, relevant published resources, practice and application prompts, social connections, and reflection. Existing content and source files are preserved.
- My Work offers an optional focus chooser and a downloadable practice guide. It does not save an employee profile, accept staff writing, or create a personal score. Individual orientation-based personalization remains a later layer.
- Staff ASK remains the existing published-topic browser, with proportionate onward guidance. The separately authorized generative agent prompt and resource index also recognize the new pathways; this does not reopen closed staff write endpoints.
- The offline SharePoint counterpart uses current scoped publication exports rather than treating recovered candidates as published. Its release receipt separately accounts for content, local media, interactions, and functions that require an online service.

## Logic and evidence

Curated knowledge, relationships, accessible tools, and time support reflection, dialogue, rehearsal, feedback, and application. Useful outputs may include a clearer question, revised interpretation, practical aid, or agreed action. Changed practice and longer-term equity contributions require their own evidence; neither a page view nor course completion establishes an outcome or individual IDI movement.

The design uses capability, opportunity, and motivation together, informed by Michie, van Stralen and West (2011), DOI 10.1186/1748-5908-6-42. Its support for constructive questions and team learning is informed by Edmondson (1999), DOI 10.2307/2666999. These sources support the design rationale, not a claim of demonstrated program effectiveness.

## Inventory and limits

The production-scoped publication export contains 240 courses and 1,415 lessons per view, with 59 shared-view resources and 85 DSD-view resources, 13 practice paths per view, 14 community-brief surfaces, and two podcast surfaces. The DSD view also has 12 program and 13 scenario surfaces. The local route inventory distinguishes 69 staff page templates from consultant, contributor, and share templates; publication checks still govern dynamic destinations.

Original lesson bodies and photographs were retained. Many existing lessons derive displayed objectives at render time; this release does not claim a new instructional-quality review or reauthoring of every lesson. Existing withheld resource rows remain withheld. Native American and Tribal-specific content and decisions continue to defer to the Office of Indian Affairs and the DHS offices responsible for Tribal matters. No new external invitations, assigned human commitments, staff outcome-data collection, or assessment scoring is activated.

## Verification and release evidence

Local checks cover compilation, TypeScript, changed-file lint, publication/scope/withdrawal behavior, existing staff write restrictions, search guidance, and a rebuilt public semantic projection. The rendered-route verification script checks every published course overview, the first lesson of every course, all published library resources, main entry points, and explicit pathway links in both views. Browser checks cover complete journeys, mobile layout, keyboard use, and downloads; offline verification has its own receipt.

See the dated release evidence for actual results and deployment identity. This implementation record alone is not evidence of production deployment or achieved staff outcomes.
