# One DHS and One DSD Program Operating Charter

Current operating direction, September 8, 2026. Internal program guidance.

One DHS People, Access and Culture is a continuing resource, learning and equity-practice program. One DSD is its originating and proving environment, as identified by Gary. The application carries part of the program's work alongside Gary's face-to-face consulting, the One DSD Team and voluntary staff participation. Its purpose is to help people apply equity, inclusion and access considerations in actual workforce, policy, program and service decisions and develop greater independence in comparable work.

## Authority and continuity

The current [DIGITAL MING owner directive](OWNER-DIRECTIVE-2026-09-07.md) governs this charter. DIGITAL MING extends Gary's professional approach through the Chief of Staff and cooperating agents. The Chief of Staff coordinates, delegates, completes routine authorized work and records actual results. Gary retains final program judgment and the ability to inspect, stop or reduce activity. Owner-curated content is already approved; this charter creates no additional content approval body.

The owner has directed activation and connection of the existing operating model. This charter consolidates its compatible requirements; it does not claim that meetings have occurred, people have accepted assignments, funding is allocated or benefits have been achieved. An operational record must establish each of those facts. Existing authorization for program work does not create official DHS, HR, case, legal or Tribal decision authority or permission to contact others outside the actual task scope.

The preserved September 4 One DSD Program Operating Model, Superior Operating Program PRD and Master PRD supply the underlying design. The September 6 Workforce and One DSD Team addendum supplies the work cycle. The September 7 owner directive supersedes their conflicting repeat-content-approval requirements, fixed autonomy ceilings and former operating-concept wording. Source material and historical versions remain intact.

## How the program produces value

Gary's expertise and relationships, the volunteer Team, approved sources, accessible learning, practical tools and cooperating agents support a connected sequence:

1. Identify a real question, barrier or work need.
2. Find relevant evidence, organizational context and resources.
3. Produce or improve a useful answer, work product, learning experience or support action.
4. Apply it in the work through the responsible person or authorized agent.
5. Review what happened and retain, revise, connect, support or stop the work.
6. Carry useful knowledge and decisions into the next comparable task.

An answer or artifact is a delivery result. Its use in actual work is application evidence. An observed improvement is benefit evidence. These are connected but separate: a successful software test, click, course completion or saved self-check does not establish improved practice. Fewer consultations alone does not establish independence. People remain free to seek support.

## Documented planning rhythm

These are preserved planning patterns, not imposed schedules or a claim that a pilot has started. Early 2027 is tentative. Gary and his colleagues will shape the operating choices; no dates, participation commitments or policy decisions are invented here.

| Planning pattern | Purpose | Evidence of actual operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly One DSD Team general meeting | Priorities, substantive learning, accepted work and follow-up | Confirmed occurrence and agreed decisions or next actions |
| Recurring Open Hours | Voluntary help with questions or work in progress | Confirmed session and appropriately shared follow-up; no invented dates |
| Quarterly review | Staff themes, resource gaps, accessibility, active work and initiative progress | Dated evidence review and recorded dispositions |
| Annual refresh | Scope, responsibilities, resources, measures, continuity and priorities | Updated operating decisions and an identified next review |

Preparing an agenda does not mean a meeting was held. An automated review is not a human meeting. Cadence definitions do not send invitations, schedule people or invent participation. Agent preparation and program reviews can proceed within their actual authorization; human occurrence and commitments require their own factual record.

## Work, measurement and improvement

Each accepted work item connects a need, scope, program function, intended outcome, task, relevant resources, evidence limits, responsible role or confirmed owner, next review and actual result. An unassigned human role stays unassigned. Failed, partial, blocked and completed work remain distinguishable. Completed agent work has a durable receipt that identifies its actual result; retries and proposals are not additional completions.

The 90–95% agent-driven target is measured against a declared eligible task set and reporting period. Report independent agent completions and assisted completions separately, together with human interventions, failures and exclusions with reasons. An empty evidence set means there is not yet evidence in that record; it does not prove no work happened elsewhere.

Participation is voluntary across DHS, ADSA and DSD. Gary has identified approximately 39 Equity Directors and other equity professionals as colleagues whose input should help shape the program. This is his account of the collaboration context, not a roster or an assignment.

The staff-record retention duration remains undecided; the previously suggested 90 days is not an accepted policy. Real staff intake must not be activated on an assumed duration or an imposed operating policy. Keep proposed collection and retention choices available for collaborative resolution, while continuing authorized research, preparation and verification that do not require real staff submissions.

Participant application and benefit evidence is voluntary. Record the source of an observation, the change described, its time period and limitations. Evaluate program support and practice, not employee beliefs or individual performance. Respect existing access, retention, correction and deletion controls; operational records do not become shared staff conversation memory.

Use the agreed review arrangements, once confirmed with the relevant colleagues, to examine practical evidence and decide what to change. Preserve capacity and continuity needs as open work until actual arrangements are confirmed; do not invent staffing, budget or sponsorship. Keep these operating details in the protected workspace and user guidance where needed, rather than filling staff pages with development discussion.
