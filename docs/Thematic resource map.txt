How to read the organization
Each receipt retains its source family and original title. Each course receives a proposed primary home. Related themes provide multiple ways to find the same material, not copies. Primary placement is a first-pass catalog judgment based on recovered family descriptions and course metadata, not a completed instructional audit of every lesson, slide, audio file, or external link.

The ten themes are review lenses, not ten required navigation tabs, learner levels, or compulsory steps. Consultant operations are separate. Audience and applicability are independent of theme: an internal record within a learning theme does not become public merely because of its placement.

INTERPRETATION AND RECOMMENDATIONS

The recovered course set is weighted toward intercultural and community understanding: 50 of 86 records have that proposed primary home. That includes 40 cultural-intelligence records plus ten related records. One Tribal Nations course is placed separately. This is a count of records, not learning hours, instructional quality, or program importance.

Structural equity has 99 ledger entries but five course records. That suggests an opportunity to connect practical tools with courses; it does not establish that structural learning is absent. Similarly, policy has five primary ledger entries and no primary course here, but obligations also appear within access and employment materials.

Workplace culture and well-being have twelve ledger entries and three courses. Amplify Equity should be explicitly represented in later reconciliation, not assumed present because a related theme exists. Its role, as Gary described it, includes voluntary discussion, wellness, cultural celebration, and staff experience that can inform better work. It is neither a performance test nor a substitute for addressing structural barriers.

Tribal sovereignty remains distinct. Cultural profiles do not replace government-to-government responsibilities. Native American community context may connect to Tribal relations without being treated as equivalent to the eleven Tribal Nations.

Keep learning methods available across all themes. A scenario, reflection prompt, brief tip, discussion, course, or checklist should use the format suited to its purpose. Not every resource needs to become a course or carry a photograph.

Preserve the seed items. Their practical introductions and checklists can serve as useful companions to deeper recovered materials. For example, the seed interpreter guide and the recovered interpreter course are potential companions; they should not be merged automatically on title similarity alone.

Titles are reproduced as found for identification. Some, including "Culture Without Theater" and "A Language Access Plan You Can Walk," warrant the agreed plain-language review. Do not treat this document as approved staff-facing copy.

CONNECTION MAP — readable workflow

A real question, staff experience, or service barrier
    -> Relevant themes and community/person context
    -> Learning, examples, and directly usable tools
    -> A decision or change in practice
    -> Feedback and review of the result
    -> Refined tools, learning, and future decisions

This is a program improvement loop, not a prerequisite sequence for accessing resources. People may go directly to any useful resource.

Connection 1: A form is difficult to complete.
Community understanding + disability and person-centered practice
    -> language/access tools and the form-change checklist
    -> equity analysis of steps, burdens, and who is missing
    -> applicable responsibilities and accountable leadership
    -> revised service experience
    -> user feedback and review of remaining barriers.

Connection 2: Recruitment attracts applicants but retention is uneven.
Hiring and employee-life-cycle resources
    -> accessibility and equitable screening
    -> mentoring, sponsorship, and onboarding
    -> staff voice and workplace climate
    -> leadership review of patterns and decisions
    -> revised hiring and retention practices.

Connection 3: A team wants to discuss an equity concern.
Workplace culture / Amplify Equity opportunity
    -> intercultural learning and facilitation
    -> optional reflection or practice scenario
    -> recurring issues considered for structural improvement
    -> communication back about what changed.
Do not turn voluntary participation or identifiable personal reflections into performance judgments.

Connection 4: A decision involves Tribal Nations.
Tribal sovereignty and the relevant official responsibilities
    -> appropriate government-to-government process
    -> service, access, and equity resources as supporting material
    -> accountable decisions and follow-through.
A general community-engagement exercise is not a substitute.

Suggested relationship labels for later design:
"Explains" — a course explains an idea used in a tool.
"Helps apply" — a checklist supports a practical action.
"Provides context" — a brief informs preparation without predicting an individual.
"Sets requirements" — an applicable authoritative source constrains a decision.
"Helps review" — feedback or evaluation helps assess an outcome.
"Related practice" — another theme offers a useful perspective.
These are proposed relationships, not newly installed application behavior.

LEARNING AND PRESENTATION RULES TO CARRY FORWARD

Each lesson should have three to five clear, observable objectives, suitable examples, and a way to practice or check understanding. This is Gary's program requirement; it is not a requirement that every checklist have five objectives.

Keep direct access to resources. Offer suggested connections rather than mandatory levels. Use inviting, accurate language that calls people into the work without disguising real responsibilities. Distinguish required practice from voluntary development and discussion.

Display scope when it matters to action: agencywide, administration, or division. Do not infer agencywide applicability merely because DSD developed a strong resource. Do not infer that other administrations lack resources because this recovered collection is DSD-rich.
