INSTRUCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS CONFIRMED WITH THE OWNER
Each lesson needs three to five meaningful learning objectives, expressed as things a learner can explain, examine, apply, or do.
The structure should be clear through headings, sequence, examples, and related materials, without public level labels or a forced progression.
Resources remain directly accessible. Optional learning does not create an artificial prerequisite for using a tool.
Use a calling-in approach: welcome questions, examine evidence and practices, and do not shame people or infer motives from group identity.
Keep actual policy requirements accurate and distinct from optional learning and discussion.
Workplace well-being and support belong alongside service outcomes and structural change, not in place of them.
Do not claim that every existing lesson meets these requirements until the lesson-level audit has been completed.
