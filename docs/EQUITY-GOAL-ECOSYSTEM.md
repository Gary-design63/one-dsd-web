# Equity goal ecosystem

The owner-approved implementation connects the six ADSA goal headings to the Equity Analysis Toolkit, published resources, organizational anti-racism learning, and manager/supervisor planning in both program views and the offline SharePoint companion.

## Source and interpretation

The goal headings come from the supplied ADSA Equity and Inclusion Implementation Plan: Eliminate Disparities; Community Engagement; Hiring and Retention; Learning and Development; Contracts and Procurement; Communication and Accessibility. The goal model retains those titles exactly. Public examples, questions and learning summaries are program adaptations, not additional agency mandates.

The Anti-Racism Resource Guide informs institutional practice, tokenism and organizational culture readings. Original source documents remain preserved separately. Identifiable focus-group reports and private email headers are not included in public assets. Draft metrics, historical dates, proposed service levels and unverified implementation claims have not been promoted into requirements.

Only the DSD equity committee is described as the One DSD Team. DHS and ADSA committee names and responsibilities remain distinct. The six goals are not presented as the official toolkit process or as an individual intercultural assessment.

## Staff experience

- All six goals appear on the homepage, Learning and the toolkit page, with discoverable links from My Work.
- Each goal connects an everyday decision, examination questions, anti-racism practice and a useful working output.
- Twenty-five connections resolve to twenty-two distinct resources. Only approved resources returned for the current staff scope are linked.
- Managers and supervisors select exactly three priorities and prepare eight action/review fields for each. The choice does not waive other responsibilities.
- This planner is newly implemented from the owner's requirement. It is not claimed to be the recovered original work-plan feature or an officially approved agency form.
- Drafts exist only in component state. Export produces a local Markdown document; nothing is submitted or saved by the program. Leaving or reloading loses unexported notes. Deselection preserves notes within the page, and clearing requires confirmation.
- Staff responsibility for required policy/toolkit use is explicit. Companion learning is optional and does not replace required agency training.

## Verification boundaries

The same goal data and interactive component are used in the application and offline SharePoint. The offline version adapts links to its hash router. Current official DHS guidance remains the authority for the appropriate type of review, documentation and approval. Companion practice questions do not claim to replace that guidance.
